Where my phone pointed, month by month

Each square is a 5 km cell where Google Maps logged me. The strip below is everything logged, by source. An algorithm split it into four phases without knowing anything about my life. Drag it.

In 2023 I wrote an essay for a law exam: what we produce online stops being ours once it's recorded. In 2026 I tested it. I asked Spotify, Instagram and Google for everything they had on me, 605,610 events over eleven years. How much can a stranger work out from the traces alone?
