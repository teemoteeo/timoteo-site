a-maze-ing

A terminal maze generator and solver. It reads a config file, carves a perfect maze with one of three algorithms and solves it with bidirectional BFS, all inside a curses TUI with threaded animations. The generator also ships on its own as the pip-installable mazegen package. Every maze has a "42" drawn in the middle.

how it works

  • Three carving algorithms: randomized DFS gives long, winding corridors. Prim grows from a frontier and gives many short dead ends. Kruskal merges sets with union-find and path compression.
  • Solver: BFS from both ends at once, meeting in the middle, so it explores far fewer cells than a one-sided search.
  • Perfect or not: a perfect maze has exactly one path between any two cells. With PERFECT=FALSE about 15% of the remaining walls are removed to create loops, and fully open 3×3 areas are broken up again.
  • Output: each cell is a hex nibble (one bit per wall), followed by entry, exit and the shortest path written as N/E/S/W.
